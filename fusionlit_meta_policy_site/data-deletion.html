Data Deletion Policy

Last updated: August 9, 2026

Fusionlit Games respects user data privacy and operates in compliance with Meta Platform Policies regarding data retention and deletion.

Data Storage

The Fusionlit Games application does not collect, track, or store personal user profile data on external servers or databases. Access tokens are used strictly to perform authorized Facebook Page video management actions.

Automatic Data Removal & Revocation

To completely disconnect the application and invalidate active authorization tokens, remove Fusionlit Games from your Facebook account settings under Apps and Websites / Business Integrations. Once removed, all permissions and access tokens are immediately invalidated.
