Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Fusionlit Games Facebook publishing application ("the App") handles information when it is used to manage content on Facebook Pages.

1. Information the App may access

When authorized by a Page administrator, the App may receive information required to manage the selected Facebook Page, including the Page name, Page ID, Page access authorization, and information needed to publish or schedule Page video content.

2. How information is used

The App uses authorized information only to provide its Facebook Page publishing functionality, including uploading, publishing, and scheduling video content on Pages selected by the administrator.

3. Information storage

Access credentials used by the App are stored in the App's private configuration environment and are not intentionally displayed publicly. The App does not sell personal information.

4. Sharing

The App does not sell or rent personal information. Information may be transmitted to Meta/Facebook as necessary to perform actions requested by the authorized Page administrator.

5. Data retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the App's functionality, maintain authorization, troubleshoot the service, or satisfy legal obligations.

6. Your choices

A Page administrator can revoke the App's access through Facebook/Meta account settings. You may also request deletion as described on our Data Deletion page.

7. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect application credentials and configuration information. No method of electronic storage or transmission is guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated when the App's functionality or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page.
